XMPlay Accessibility 1.1.0

This add-on makes the custom-skinned XMPlay interface usable with NVDA. It reads playback changes automatically and provides a standard accessible control center containing playback status, a searchable playlist, selected-file details, transport controls, volume controls, track information, and accessible file pickers.

Getting started

While XMPlay has focus, press NVDA+Shift+X. The accessible control center opens with focus in the playlist search box. Press Tab to reach the playlist, selected-track details, and all controls. Press Enter or use the Play selected button to play the highlighted track. Escape closes the window.

Information commands

  • NVDA+Shift+X: accessible control center.
  • NVDA+Shift+P: accessible playlist.
  • NVDA+Shift+I: current title and playlist position.
  • NVDA+Shift+T: elapsed, total, and remaining time.
  • NVDA+Shift+S: complete playback status.
  • NVDA+Shift+V: volume and balance.
  • NVDA+Shift+G: general track information.
  • NVDA+Shift+M: message and tags.
  • NVDA+Shift+A: module samples and instruments.
  • NVDA+Shift+D: all track-information pages.
  • NVDA+Shift+O: all detectable text in the current XMPlay window, useful in custom dialogs.

Playback commands

  • Control+Shift+Space: play or pause.
  • Control+Shift+S: stop.
  • Control+Shift+Left/Right Arrow: previous or next track.
  • Control+Shift+Page Up/Page Down: seek backward or forward.
  • Control+Shift+Up/Down Arrow: volume up or down.
  • Control+Shift+M: mute or restore volume.
  • Control+Shift+L: cycle the track-loop mode.

Settings

Open NVDA Settings and select the XMPlay category. Every automatic announcement can be enabled or disabled separately: the welcome message, track changes, playback state, volume, balance, help bubbles, keyboard-command feedback, and control-center confirmations. Manually requested reports and errors remain available at all times. The Restore defaults button changes all checkboxes back to their original values; press OK or Apply to save.

All keyboard commands can be changed or removed in NVDA's Input Gestures dialog, in the XMPlay category.

Notes

The add-on communicates with XMPlay through its documented DDE commands and its Winamp-compatible status interface, so it works independently of the selected skin. XMPlay itself has no language-pack mechanism; the add-on's accessible interface and spoken messages follow NVDA's language. English and Polish are included.
